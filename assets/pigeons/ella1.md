---
title: Home
subtitle: Thoughts on...
date: 1 October 2025
place: Barcelona, Catalunya
language: Ella Suzanne
---

I live on the fourth floor of an apartment block, with a balcony arching over a gentle street where my view is mostly that of other balconies. One down and to the left of mine, I had never looked before. There was no asking for attention. Yet when this seemingly abandoned tiled floor and floral curtains was chosen, it became the view. It was chosen by a pair of pigeons to, through the course of weeks in the summer of 2025, build their home.

I watched them, together with anyone who came over. I´d watch one pigeon keep the eggs warm in their young nest as the other flew back and forth for new branches. Every first wing flap towards retrieving new material, would slightly wind the latest branches out of place. Sometimes the sweet pigeon would place a branch on the other, almost blanketing them. The nest felt out of place and bare, but the spread of layered bird shit covering the tiles showed this wasn't the first season of homing for pigeons on this balcony.

As I settled into my new home on my new balcony in my new city, the pair cared their little eggs in hatching. The babies were held there for a while until one day they had all flown away. All that's left is the spread of shit, and the nest of branches that did keep.

I have grown to feel a kinship with pigeons. They seem hungry for a home; for things to build a home. What does their natural home even consist of? Because when I look at them, for a while longer than liminal glances, floating through low skies across city plazas, they seem lost. Perhaps that is just a projection; an animal out of context.
That's often how I´ve been feeling; out of context, in a city.
